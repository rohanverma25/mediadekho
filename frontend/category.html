Everything You Need to Know About Magazine Advertising

Discover how magazine advertising builds trust, connects brands with targeted high-net-worth audiences, and delivers unmatched engagement.

Targeted Niche Audience Reach

Magazines cater to specific demographics and interest segments—whether trade professionals (Architects, Doctors, Engineers), business leaders (CXOs, HNI investors), or luxury consumers. Your advertisement reaches readers actively seeking specialized insights.

Extended Shelf Life & High Recall

Unlike ephemeral digital ads or daily newspapers, glossy print magazines are kept on coffee tables, executive desks, and lounge waiting rooms for months. Each copy is read repeatedly by multiple readers, multiplying campaign impressions.

Unmatched Brand Credibility

Appearing in acclaimed publications like Vogue, Forbes, or Business Today transfers prestige and authority to your brand. Print publication ads foster deep consumer trust, making high-ticket purchasing decisions smoother.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about advertising rates, ad specs, and booking timelines.

What are the rates for Magazine Advertising in India?
Magazine advertising rates vary based on publication reach, circulation, page position, and ad size. Standard full-page color ads range from ₹50,000 for specialized trade magazines to ₹5,000,000+ for premium national covers like Vogue or Forbes. Media Dekho provides guaranteed direct media owner rates with zero hidden markups.
How do I book an ad spot through Media Dekho?
Booking is fast and seamless: simply select your target magazine, add your preferred ad placement (Full Page, Inside Cover, Back Cover) to your campaign plan, and submit an inquiry. Our media buying strategist will confirm availability, lock in your spot, and guide you through creative artwork submission.
What artwork specifications are required for magazine print ads?
Print ads require high-resolution (minimum 300 DPI) artwork formatted in CMYK color mode. Accepted formats include vector PDF, Adobe Illustrator (.ai), or high-res TIFF with 3mm to 5mm bleed space. Our design team audits every artwork prior to publication printing.
Will I receive physical proof of publication (voucher copy)?
Yes! Media Dekho provides 100% geotagged proof of execution along with 2 physical original voucher issue copies shipped directly to your corporate headquarters immediately after printing.
How far in advance should I book my magazine ad spot?
We recommend booking your ad spot at least 15 to 20 days before the publication month's deadline. Premium positions like Inside Covers (IFC/IBC) or Back Cover (BC) sell out quickly and should be locked in 30 days prior.
