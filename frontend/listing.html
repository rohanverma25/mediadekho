Architect and Interiors India FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking your advertisement.

What is the circulation and readership of Architect and Interiors India?
Architect & Interiors India has a verified print circulation of 45,000 copies per monthly issue, reaching over 180,000 readers across top architectural firms, interior design consultancies, and real estate developers in India.
What is the deadline for submitting artwork for an upcoming issue?
Ad space bookings and final print artwork files must be submitted by the 15th of the preceding month (e.g. for the October issue, artwork approval must be completed by September 15th).
What file formats and print specifications are required?
Artwork should be supplied as press-ready CMYK PDF or TIFF format with 300 DPI resolution. Full page dimension is 205 mm x 275 mm with a 3mm bleed on all sides.
Will I receive physical proof of insertion after publication?
Yes! Media Dekho delivers 2 physical original voucher copies of the published magazine issue along with a digital audit report directly to your office address within 7 days of distribution.
Can Media Dekho offer discounts for multiple issue bookings?
Yes! We offer series discounts for multi-issue packages (3-issue, 6-issue, or 12-issue annual campaign packages). Contact our media strategist for custom package pricing.
