Cart & Proposal FAQs

Learn how Media Dekho handles campaign quotes, payments, and execution proof.

What happens after I submit my campaign plan request?
Once you request an official proposal quote, a dedicated Media Dekho Strategist reviews media inventory availability, verifies final print/display deadlines, and sends a formal PDF proposal along with payment details within 15 minutes.
Are these prices final or subject to negotiation?
Media Dekho aggregates official direct owner rates with zero hidden agent markups. For bulk multi-channel or annual campaigns, our team can unlock additional publisher series discounts up to 15%.
When do I need to submit my campaign artwork?
Artwork deadlines depend on the media type (15-20 days before publication month for glossy trade magazines, 5-7 days for digital LED screens and airport billboards). Our team will provide exact specs and templates after booking.
How is campaign execution verified?
We provide 100% verified geotagged proof photos and physical original issue voucher copies delivered straight to your corporate address immediately upon campaign launch.
